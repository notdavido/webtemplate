Autobiography in prose

David Vytopil

When I was a child, I drew a boat, except I failed to realize I had the sail upside down. The same child who would eventually become a top typist, university violinist, trilingualist, coder… etc. Now, I look at the boat with an upside down sail, and I see more than just a failed drawing, I see a creation, one that, despite being built incorrectly or being broken, still sails the sea and wears it as though a badge of confidence. I would venture to say I was a very unintelligent child, I had no skills or strengths, I was free and naive. The most noteworthy things that happened to me were being put in a gifted class in 4th grade-- which was taken back next year, and then being recruited and joining AVID starting 6th grade.

I will briefly mention here that there are many things that I will not go into detail on but will somewhat list the nature of them here. My childhood is not defined by physical fights, but things have gone physical with the male figures in my life, even if not a full on fight. There have been many times I have been brought out to yell in a way I never knew I could. There are multiple occasions of police intervention. There are multiple sleepless nights where the only thing I focus on is lowering my adrenaline. There are multiple times I have given a light story to the guardian ad litem or guidance counselors. There are multiple occasions of severe internal critique and dissociation.

Between 5th and 6th grade, there was a stark change in my personality, and I would venture to say that it was when I started to lose my naivety, though it's also just as likely due to parent marriage problems. In 6th grade, I hardly talked to anyone for the entire year, no friends, many missing assignments, addicted to videogames. It was a miracle I kept AVID. I joined the orchestra on a whim but didn't care about the violin and hardly practiced at all throughout my middle school years. Somehow I was able to beat the 6th grade record for holding a plank and was neck and neck with the 6th grade pacer record as well, it was the most human I felt in that entire year. The teacher would often ask what I did when I got home to try to make sense of the lack of completed assignments, but I wouldn’t answer, because I felt I genuinely went home and did nothing.

7th and 8th, due to repeated exposure with classmates, I started to warm up just a little bit, but I was still incredibly shallow. My mother obviously cared for me, but is misguided and often any small thing could turn into a psychologically abusive multi hour talk where what she really wants is pure dominance. One day, a huge fight with my mother over my laptop happened, which probably held 90% of my worth as a human at the time. She kicked me out, said she was changing locks. So I lived with my father for a while, before she changed her mind and took me home directly from the school one day, who knows if she only did it when she did because she was worried my father would use it to seal the custody arrangement. And the start of 8th grade was admittedly a turning point of my life, I learned to solve the rubix cube and I used it to show off in my homeroom ‘get to know you’ activities. My after school experience would generally be streaming a random roblox game to gain a youtube following, and the hours were honestly intense and lead to burnout in a year. But even so, today I still have some of my most loyal online friends in a discord server that I met from streaming, and they are worth all the effort, even if at times I would withdraw and leave my own server for months at times.

And that is where it began, I learned an unusual skill and felt the hit of self worth that had been inherently lacking or failing to grow my entire life.

But the pessimism still grew and the Titan story happened.

8th grade cut short for COVID, 9th grade COVID year. There were only two people I talked to throughout this time, an online friend and a previous school friend, both are precious to me even if I was not who they needed me to be. I learned to type, obsessed over it, because every higher score was a small numbness to the emptiness of my self worth. I still played a ton of video games, it was numbing, but videogames did not give me a good feeling because subconsciously a feeling of needing to do something to prove my worth would grow, hence the typing or the cube.

10th, I met one of my longest standing best friends, we were similar in that we changed interests a lot, but different in that I hyper focused my identity on being good at them.

And then chess happened, and admittedly chess is one of the worst things someone like me can get into. A fragile sense of worth, fluctuating with the hundreds of points on a screen with each win and loss. The only thing that separated me from being egotistical was that I felt the lows of losses to my soul, and it extended to every hobby.

11th, I managed to rejoin the orchestra, I was building a slow and real passion for the violin, and even took private lessons at this point.

Home life was still chaotic and unstable, the closer I got to being 18 the worse the problems got. I was never at risk of homelessness, but I was always at risk of being stranded or given up to the other parent. As such, during a hearing for parental battle of custody, I was officially declared emancipated, before I was 18. My online emotional support became strained and I became more grounded with the people around me and I became more and more competitive on the outcomes of events. A few months before high school graduation (I was still 17) another big conflict with mother, it just wouldn’t deescalate, it was the day I needed to present in a mock court trial for our ap government class, I needed to go to school late to prepare some last minute things for our group, I was taking it seriously. But all she heard was defiance, so no deescalation, took away my ‘car privilege’ and stormed off to the school. I plugged the car battery back in and drove and managed, once again, to complete the mock trial just satisfactorily enough with no one noticing anything off. Later that day my mother continued back at 100, I ended up throwing my phone at the wall, but the psychological barrage still didn’t stop and I punched the wall as well. Dispatch arrives and I go to my fathers once more. I did not talk to her for months, even when she tried to fix things with gifts at my graduation. It was actually a huge surprise that I managed to graduate, because I went into that final year selecting nearly all AP classes, but even from the beginning I had no intention of doing the work for any of them, but come mid year I realized I still needed a few credits and barely managed to scrape out a few grades in the yearlong classes. I failed my first class here, calculus. I did not do any homework assignments and only did the minimum work in class to meet ‘compliance’ so that the teacher could not call security or something. And yet, I still managed to graduate with honors, and managed to get admitted into ODU.

The first year of University, I started living on campus in the second semester. I got away. I thought I was curing the performance based metrics, but I didn't. I managed to get into CLS spark, managed to win a division 26 CCL, managed to compete in a 5k, and managed a 17 credit hour semester, and started getting serious about running. There is no off switch.

Summer housing was a bit tight so I lived with a parent again for a summer. Objectively the worst summer of my life, I lost a ton of money on the stock market and failed two classes, got kicked out of the house again then got led on by a girl who eventually abandoned me as though I was air. But still, I took out a top step combine certificate, and I passed the CLS program. I don’t know where my fight comes from sometimes, but that sliver is always being directly challenged by someone or something every time things start looking optimistic. Do not blame me for not wanting to clean the laundry room like your dog on command, I am protecting the last bit of respect I have for myself that you keep trying to blow up, because deep down, you know what you want in a child is a shallow shell people pleaser.

The stock market reopened the story I was learning from chess, the problem was so clear, I would chase trades and my entire mood for a week could be determined by a trade, I simply could not make a trade and not base my worth on it. Today I am currently in a longstanding battle to erase this attachment. I trade once or twice a week on topstep, never double trade or chase, and admittedly I choose an up or down bias before trading to make the attachment easier to dismiss.

Opening up to someone wasn't easy but I had done it, being abandoned as though I didn't matter reopened almost every childhood wound and the performance based identity kicked on at max, causing me to fully create this website that is still expanding. It is admittedly difficult for me to understand their cowardice when I'd lived half my life fighting tooth and nail for the will to simply care about the next day. Maybe if I didn’t have talent or that last sliver of self protection I sometimes have no reason to fight for, maybe I would have grown into something similar, someone who seeks pure social validation, or substance abuse, or a shallow and conflict avoidant withdrawing type of person. I am thankful for what I do have, but I do not believe this fight is impossible for everyone else, I find it hard to respect sometimes. It is hard, there is definitely survivor’s guilt here, and sometimes seeing the way other people are shows me just how different I could have been without that fight, or maybe that fight is what makes the forfeit that much worse. I’m not sure.

Bigger and better achievements are working less and less and the hollowness is catching up more and more. My motive is to finally put my performance based identity to rest and find real people who don’t sacrifice others for internal comfort because that is the most hurtful thing someone trying to heal like me can deal with, and the solution is not to wear my mask and keep people away. It is a fight. The website is a result of me being pushed to the limit of fighting for my worth, and it is both a blessing and a curse.

In the past half year, I’ve rented a yearlong lease, managed to get high view videos on youtube, did an 18 credit hour semester and then rejoined the symphony and private lessons on top of that, joined Liquid Talent Agency, swapped majors, tried out for JV Valorant team (pending results), contacted a friend for competitive running advice to potentially join a running team, because I’ve moved from being fine with a new pb on a typing website to now suffocating hollowness even with institutional validation on completely random fields, it’s becoming less effective. And yet, now I am chasing after a Virginian Pilot publication, with a deadline of before being 20. Because there is no off switch.

I am not asking for your sympathy, there were hundreds of ways I could have asked. I only want to be understood and redefine myself in a stable manner, in a way where I can be socially awkward but not misunderstood.

Former computer science major, psychology major with music performance and computer science minors, symphony violinist, trilingualist, Irmonus, signing off.
